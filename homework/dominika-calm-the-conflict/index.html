Part 1

Which type of conflict is it?

Of interestNot enough time, people, space or shifts.
Of misunderstandingSame goal, different angles or different words.
InterpersonalPersonal: tone, ego, “I don’t like how she talks to me.”
Part 2

Which style is the manager using?

CompetingSafety, tight deadlines. “Do it now.”
CollaboratingImportant, repeating problems. “What do you each need?”
CompromisingNot much time. Each side gets half.
AvoidingPeople are too angry to talk. “Let’s talk later.”
AccommodatingThe relationship matters more. “Let’s do it your way.”

Which answer sounds calmer?
