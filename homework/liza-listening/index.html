01

Before you start

The five habits from the lesson. Keep them in mind in every task.

Habit 1

Read first.

Look at the questions before you press play. Predict the type of answer: a number, a name, a noun.

Habit 2

Expect synonyms.

The answer is usually said in different words from the question.

Habit 3

Wait for the change.

"Sorry", "actually", "no, wait": the first thing you hear is often wrong.

Habit 4

Check the small stuff.

Spelling, plurals, numbers and capital letters can cost points.

New: the player

Press the square button to play or pause. Pause keeps your place. Click or drag the bar to jump. The ±5 s buttons move a little. Thin marks on the bar show where each speaker's turn begins.
