09

Role-play: the late response

The deadline is tomorrow and the response to a deficiency letter is not ready. Play it for four minutes, then swap roles.

Speak in pairs

A · Head of Regulatory Affairs

  • You need the full picture today.
  • Ask how many questions are answered and how long each part has taken.
  • Find out what is blocking the team and why.
  • Ask for a plan and a firm date.

B · Regulatory Affairs specialist

  • You have answered 14 of 20 questions.
  • You've been waiting five days for stability data from QA.
  • You haven't received the final labelling from the printer yet.
  • Suggest a solution and ask for 48 hours more.
Useful phrases
I'm afraid we haven't…
So far, we've…
We've been waiting for… for five days.
The main issue is that…
Could you give us until…?
What I suggest is…
10

Role-play: a call from the authority

Formal register. A reviewer calls with questions about a submission. Stay polite, be precise, and don't promise what you can't do.

Speak in pairs

A · Reviewer at the authority

  • The stability data arrived two weeks late. Ask why.
  • Ask what the company has done to avoid this.
  • Ask whether the product has already been released on the market.
  • End by saying when you need the missing document.

B · Company representative

  • The data was delayed because the lab changed the method (Past Simple).
  • You have updated the procedure since then (Present Perfect).
  • The product hasn't been released yet.
  • You will send the missing document by Friday.
Formal phrases
I'd like to clarify that…
To date, we have…
I can confirm that…
Since then, we have been…

We will provide it by…

RESULT

have / has + V3
  • done, it matters now
  • how many, how much
  • already, yet, so far
  • We've answered 12 questions.

PROCESS

have / has been + V-ing
  • the activity itself
  • how long, since, for, all week
  • a trace you can see or feel
  • We've been waiting since March.

PAST SIMPLE

did / V2
  • finished, known time
  • yesterday, last week, in 2023
  • We submitted it on Monday.

PAST CONTINUOUS

was / were + V-ing
  • a moment in the past
  • often interrupted: when / while
  • I was uploading when it crashed.
